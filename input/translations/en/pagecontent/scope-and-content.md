This implementation guide describes the DIGOVO dataset for documenting patients with rare diseases in an EHR. Its structure follows **RD_Data_Elements_CZ_EHR_VFN_16_9_VS.xlsx**, worksheet Data Elements.

The [overall logical model](StructureDefinition-LogRareDiseaseDatasetCz.html) contains 12 sections covering consent, patient identification, diagnosis, disease history, care, research and useful information. Patient information includes separate branches for personal information (2a) and foetal and maternal data (2b).

The [logical model overview](logical-models.html) presents the hierarchy, links to individual models, dataset alignment and open questions. These models are drafts; inconsistencies in the source and conditional requirements still need clarification.

Items highlighted in red in the source dataset (3.7, 7.1 and 7.3) carry the label **UNDER REVIEW – CURRENTLY INVALID** in this IG. They are retained for discussion and do not represent currently valid requirements.
