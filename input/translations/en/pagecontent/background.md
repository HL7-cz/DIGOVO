### Background

DIGOVO describes a dataset for documenting patients with rare diseases. Its logical models follow **RD_Data_Elements_CZ_EHR_VFN_16_9_VS.xlsx**, worksheet Data Elements.

The dataset contains 78 items in 12 main sections. Section 2 includes personal and demographic information (2a) and foetal and maternal data (2b). The [logical model overview](logical-models.html) presents the hierarchy and unresolved source questions.

Items 3.7, 7.1 and 7.3 are **UNDER REVIEW – CURRENTLY INVALID**. The models are working drafts.
